=== VERDICTS ===

Topic A (dendritic / burst credit assignment):
1. Frontier has not moved on the two asked questions: no burst/dendritic method is on hardware, and the burst line has no confirmed CIFAR-10/ImageNet number beyond Payeur 2021 and BurstCCN 2022 on any page I could read. The dendritic line's best confirmed number is DLL (ICML 2025) 70.89 CIFAR-10 / Tiny-ImageNet error 82.9 vs BP 78.6, non-spiking, transported error. The strongest NEW signal is indirect: the BurstCCN-journal repo (June/Aug 2026) ships CIFAR-10 and ImageNet configs, so a number probably exists in the bioRxiv full text.
2. The lineage's own 2026 papers (Safaai, von Hünerbein, He) converge on the same admission: local eligibility is fine, but accuracy depends on a transported error reaching the dendrite; "restricted feedback is the main limit" (Safaai). None pass the two-neuron bar.
3. Single most important paper: Greedy et al. BurstCCN 2026 (bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.06.16.732595) - read the full text when bioRxiv unblocks; it is the only one that could change the scorecard. Runner-up for hardware path: Stuck/Naud 2025 (MNIST 97.2 spiking, 4-layer 95.9, CPU only).

Topic B (continual learning with local rules):
1. Frontier moved in deployment, not in rule locality: Loihi 2 now runs an end-to-end continual pipeline on event-camera actions (CLANE, 70.4% on 50 classes) on top of CLP-SNN (OpenLORIS 90.0% 25-shot, 6600x energy vs edge GPU). Both learn only a prototype head with a frozen backbone, so "on-chip continual learning" still means "on-chip readout learning".
2. Pure no-gradient multi-layer SNNs: Attar (Sep 2026) reports 62.5% CIFAR-10 vs the paper's own STDP/R-STDP baseline 33.3 (no surrogate-gradient baseline reported; I did not establish 33% as the prior frontier) and SADP reaches 86.5% MNIST with one hidden layer; still far below surrogate-gradient. Every stronger "local" result (BSD 84.1 CIFAR-10, CLAPP++ ImageNet 42.6, GHL ImageNet 73.1) is within-layer gradients or needs a BP sign, and none is spiking except BSD. Watch for retitled "gradient-free" papers (ISI-CV) where only the regulariser is gradient-free.
3. Single most important paper: Hajizada et al. CLP-SNN (arXiv 2511.01553, v2 May 2026) - its rule dw = alpha * s^r * (x - w*y) is the only confirmed strictly two-neuron-local rule with an on-chip continual demo and accuracy within 1.6 pp of GPU replay at 25-shot; the frozen-backbone and imprint-once caveats are exactly the gap this project's EP line could fill.
