Verdicts
EP: Frontier has moved relative to the vault's recorded "~88% CIFAR-10" (Hopfield-Resnet13 93.92%; full ImageNet top-5 86.8% via Kerjan; spiking EP 81.3% CIFAR-10 via StochEP), but every one of those predates 21 July. Post-July EP papers are substrate and physics (PotentialEP generative, two-state synapses, Lock-in EP): no new classification number. Single most important: Kerjan/Hoier/Scellier (field level); for this project's spiking emphasis, StochEP.
PC: Moved before the survey too (ICLR 2026 Meta-PCN and DiffPC; WF-Act-PC 93.6% CIFAR-10 on VGG-9, above tuned BP). Post-July the only paper aimed at analog/neuromorphic substrates is Innocenti's Bregman PC (removes the activation derivative, costs ~0.5-1.6 pp). Single most important: DiffPC (the only spiking PC at a top venue, >100x less communication); Bregman PC is the one to read for an on-chip rule.
Cost claims: EP 12x wall-clock (Kerjan) and ~16x per step (PotentialEP) are the only measured EP-vs-BP figures; PC offers only complexity claims (DKP-PC O(1) error delay, PC-ALM T = 2L, FRE-RNN 10x fewer steps). No paper in either family reports a FLOPs-matched comparison.
