## Backprop reference (surrogate gradient; each from that paper's own table)
- DVS128 Gesture: TDFormer [SDT-2-256] 99.65% at T=16, claimed SOTA (arXiv 2505.15840v2, 2025-05-23); Spike-driven Transformer 99.3% at T=16 (arXiv 2307.01694 Table 5); NESTformer g3 99.30% (arXiv 2605.13869, 2026-05-04); QKFormer 98.6% T=16 (arXiv 2403.16552).
- CIFAR10-DVS: TDFormer [HST-2-256] 85.83% at T=16, claimed SOTA (arXiv 2505.15840v2); QKFormer 84.0% T=16 / 83.8% T=10, 1.50M params (arXiv 2403.16552 own table); NESTformer g3 83.61% (arXiv 2605.13869).

## Verdict A
- Not moved for the caller's bar. No 2025-26 paper reports a DVS-Gesture or DVS-CIFAR10 number with a strictly two-neuron-local rule; the best "local" numbers (TESS 98.56 / TP 98.18 / pp-prop 97.29 on Gesture; TESS 75.0 on CIFAR10-DVS vs 85.83 backprop) all carry a per-neuron learning signal or a layer-wise loss. Best strictly-local Gesture figure confirmed: PCL iSTDP features + SVM 89.12 (~10 pp below backprop); on N-MNIST the new MDTF STDP/R-STDP stack gives 95.0 vs ~98.3-98.4 for BPTT/pp-prop.
- No on-chip-learning demo on event data on Speck, Loihi 2, SpiNNaker2 or Akida in 2025-26: the closest are CLP-SNN (Loihi 2, RGB, last layer only) and SpiNNaker2 (e-prop/EventProp on keyword spotting, DVS inference only).
- Single most important: Wang et al. BrainTrace/pp-prop (Nat Comms, Jan 2026) sets the cost frontier (BPTT accuracy on DVS Gesture / N-MNIST / SHD at O(H) memory, constant in T) and makes the gap explicit: what remains non-local is only the spatial learning signal. NeuroTrain (May 2026) is the harness to test a strictly local rule against all of the above on the same datasets.

## B. BPU shuffled-wiring control
- BPU paper (arXiv 2507.10951): v1 only (2025-07-15); no shuffled/rewired control.
- FOUND (not peer-reviewed): cqw-acq/connectome-null-models (GitHub; listed on flybrain.info, 2026-09-12). Larval connectome (Winding 2023; 2,956 neurons, 63,545 axon->dendrite synapses), frozen recurrent matrix, trained input/output projections, explicitly the BPU setup, spectral radius 0.95. MNIST (n=5): connectome 97.30+-0.12; degree-preserving shuffle 97.28+-0.21 (+0.03 pp, p=0.80); weight-shuffle 97.38+-0.13; Erdos-Renyi 97.53+-0.30; no recurrence 11.35. CIFAR-10 (n=15): 43.04+-0.40 vs 42.95+-0.37 (+0.09 pp, p=0.51, CI [-0.20,+0.38]); BPU's 58% not reproduced. README cites BPU and states it ran no rewired control. https://github.com/cqw-acq/connectome-null-models
- Secondary: Himanshu-639/flybrain_MNIST (MaleCNS right-medulla subset, 500 LIF, 19,455 synapses, logistic readout): real 75.5% vs degree-matched random 77.0% vs raw 50-D input 81.0%. https://github.com/Himanshu-639/flybrain_MNIST
- Adjacent peer-track: Dhiman arXiv 2604.04033 (flyvis, no MNIST; connectome advantage vanishes under a degree-preserving null); Cho arXiv 2610.10014 (MaleCNS, addition/language tasks, rewires lose ~25 pp; no MNIST).
